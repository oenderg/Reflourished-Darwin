**Reflourished 1.5.0.** The game is now PvZ2: Reflourished 1.5.0 on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows, with the mod's first hotfix (1.5.0-R00) included. Your saves carry over.

- The game's data is new: after updating, the first start downloads it once (1.4 GB). The full downloads already carry it.
- Seed packets 9 and 10 have keys, for the levels that give you that many: `5` and `T` by default. Change them in Settings > Keyboard; keys you set yourself stay as they are.
- The app has the mod's new icon.
- iPhone and iPad: a tap made while another finger is down now reaches the game.
- Windows: after a seed key, the plant at the pointer now shows up reliably.
